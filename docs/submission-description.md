# Week 2 Submission Description

This Week 2 report documents a master data management and inventory optimization project using SAP Supply Chain Management concepts, with emphasis on SAP APO and Supply Network Planning. I continued the fictional Deccan Harvest Foods Pvt. Ltd. scenario from Week 1 so that the master-data decisions remain connected to the same suppliers, manufacturing plant, regional distribution centers, and customer markets.

The project reviews planning master data that affects inventory performance, including products, locations, location-products, resources, production and procurement sources, transportation lanes, calendars, lead times, units of measure, safety stock, service levels, and lot-size parameters. It presents a project plan for data profiling, cleansing, standardization, source-to-target mapping, integration, validation, migration, and ongoing governance.

The report also explains inventory optimization methods such as safety stock, safety days of supply, reorder-point analysis, EOQ, ABC-XYZ segmentation, lot-size review, redeployment, and dual sourcing. Two practical simulations are included: a stock-out risk caused by transport delay and an excess-inventory situation caused by lower demand and oversized replenishment. For each scenario, likely causes and corrective actions are discussed.

The final sections define inventory and data-quality KPIs, validation checks, ownership, risk controls, change management, and post-implementation monitoring. All values and results are illustrative and are presented as an internship planning simulation rather than a claim of live SAP production-system execution.
